Tidey Remote

Use Tidey from your iPhone.

Tidey Remote is the iPhone companion for Tidey on Mac. Pair with a QR code, follow your workspaces, chat with agent panels, review files, and send image attachments while the terminal keeps running on your Mac.

Get Tidey for Mac Install the Mac app first, then open Settings → Remote to pair your iPhone. App support and FAQ Pairing steps, permissions, remote connection notes, and support contact.
Tidey Remote requires Tidey running on a Mac.
